I'm a Canadian engineer currently working on mechatronics, firmware and controls at AON3D. We build industrial 3D printers used by aerospace companies for aircraft, rockets, satellites, and space probes, and by R&D teams in other industries. It's great because it brings together two things I enjoy: mechanical systems and low-level computing. Over the course of my career so far, I've moved from web development to robotics (for warehouse and factory automation) to mechatronics.

I did a master's degree in mechanical and aerospace engineering, but a lot of what I've learned has come from textbooks and open online courses. I enjoy working through them, both for fun and with future goals in mind. I also have a page of engineering resources that I've particularly enjoyed and recommend to anyone interested in mechanical or aerospace engineering.

I'm interested in aerospace, especially fluid dynamics, compressible flow, hypersonics, and propulsion. I've long thought nuclear rocket propulsion is essential if we're going to do ambitious space exploration, and I'd like to contribute to that research someday. Until then, I'm building the background for it through my own study.

Outside of work I like getting outdoors: hiking, cycling, kayaking, rock climbing, and photography (flickr). And, as a Canadian, naturally, hockey.
